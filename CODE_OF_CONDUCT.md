# Code of Conduct

## Our promise

Everyone who takes part in this project, in any role, deserves to feel
welcome. We are committed to keeping this a friendly, respectful and
constructive place for people of every background, identity and experience
level.

This applies to the issue tracker, pull requests, code review, and any other
space officially connected to the project.

## Expected behavior

- **Be kind and patient.** Many people here are learning. Questions of any
  level are welcome.
- **Assume good intent.** Read charitably, ask before judging, and remember
  that tone is easy to misread in text.
- **Stay constructive.** Critique ideas and code, not people. When something
  is wrong, explain what you would do instead, or say what you expected.
- **Keep it on topic.** Keep discussions focused on the project so everyone
  can follow along.
- **Respect boundaries.** If someone asks a discussion to move on, let it.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks, in public or private.
- Discriminatory jokes or language about someone's identity, including (but
  not limited to) gender, sexuality, race, religion, nationality or
  disability.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Deliberate disruption of discussions, trolling, or bad-faith arguments.
- Any other conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate
  in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies within all project spaces and also applies
when an individual is officially representing the project in other places.

## Enforcement

Problems are handled by the project maintainer (@AldySan).

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it by
[opening an issue](https://github.com/AldySan/Remote-Package-Installer-NG/issues)
marked as a conduct report, or by contacting the maintainer directly on
GitHub. All reports are taken seriously and handled with discretion.

Consequences depend on the situation and may range from a warning to a
permanent ban from project spaces.
